## 1. Document Purpose

This document defines the **approved website content and copy direction** for the professional portfolio of **Muhammad Azis Saputra**.

---

# 2. Content Principles

All content must follow the rules below.

## 2.1 Evidence Over Claims

Prefer verifiable evidence over generic statements.

### Avoid

> Experienced in Machine Learning.

### Prefer

> Developed and benchmarked computer-vision models for soybean research, including YOLO-based seed detection and real-time web deployment.

---

## 2.2 Concise but Meaningful

The website must not duplicate the full CV.

Content should be:

- Easy to scan
- Outcome-oriented
- Specific
- Professional
- Human
- Technically credible

---

## 2.3 No Unsupported Superlatives

Avoid unsupported claims such as:

- Expert
- Highly skilled
- World-class
- Advanced developer
- Industry-leading
- Best-in-class

Use factual language instead.

---

## 2.4 First-Person Voice

The portfolio should use **first-person professional language** for personal sections.

Example:

> I build practical digital solutions across software, data, and artificial intelligence.

Project cards may use neutral descriptive language.

---

## 2.5 Language

Primary website language:

**English**

Tone:

- Professional
- Clear
- Modern
- Confident
- Grounded
- Not overly formal
- Not overly casual

Avoid:

- Corporate buzzword overload
- Excessive jargon
- Motivational clichés
- AI-generated sounding phrases
- Overuse of em dashes
